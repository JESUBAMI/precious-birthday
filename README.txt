PRECIOUS BIRTHDAY WEBSITE
=========================
Files: index.html (open this), style.css, script.js, images/, videos/.
Keep them together in one folder.

Test on your computer: unzip, then double-click index.html.

Put it online free (Netlify Drop):
  1. On a laptop or PC, open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up (free).
  2. Drag the whole unzipped folder (the one containing index.html) onto the page.
  3. Netlify gives you a link ending in .netlify.app
  4. In site settings, rename the site, e.g. precious-birthday.

The wish button sends messages to Precious on WhatsApp.
To change the WhatsApp number for the wish button:
  Open script.js, find  var waNum='...'  and put the new number there
  (international format, no plus sign or spaces).
